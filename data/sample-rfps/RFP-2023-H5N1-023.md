**Program Area:** Influenza — Pandemic Preparedness

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

H5N1 highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI) has caused unprecedented poultry losses in the United States since 2022, with confirmed human cases in agricultural workers representing a critical signal for pandemic preparedness. The U.S. government has accelerated preparedness activities under the National Pandemic Influenza Plans, and state public health laboratories are essential to early detection of human-to-human transmission and case confirmation.

State LRN-B laboratories authorized for influenza A H5 testing currently rely on CDC's influenza laboratory for confirmatory testing. This RFP funds a prioritized subset of state laboratories to implement CDC-authorized H5N1 testing assays, establish agricultural worker surveillance partnerships, and conduct surge exercises simulating multi-state H5N1 case events.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- LRN-B Reference or Sentinel laboratory with BSL-2+ influenza testing capability
- State with documented H5N1 HPAI in poultry flocks OR significant dairy/poultry agricultural workforce
- Current CDC-authorized seasonal influenza testing using rRT-PCR
- Agreement to implement CDC InFLUenza Patient-Centered Outcomes Research (iHUB) reporting
- Priority given to states with agricultural worker populations and documented H5N1 poultry exposure risk

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. H5N1 Testing Implementation
- Receive CDC training and implement CDC Influenza A H5 rRT-PCR assay under EUA
- Validate H5N1 specimen processing under BSL-2+ enhanced precautions (full PPE, ventilation controls)
- Achieve capacity to process minimum 20 H5N1 suspect specimens per day within 30 days of assay receipt from CDC

### B. Agricultural Worker Surveillance
- Establish formal surveillance partnership with state agriculture department and at least 3 poultry or dairy farm operations
- Develop occupational exposure monitoring protocol: syndromic surveillance, respiratory specimen collection, serology
- Train farm health officers and agricultural extension staff in specimen collection and biosafety

### C. Surge Exercise and Interoperability
- Conduct at least one full-scale functional exercise simulating simultaneous H5N1 cases in 3 counties within period of performance
- Conduct interoperability exercise with neighboring state LRN laboratory for surge overflow testing
- Submit exercise after-action report to Public Health Labs and CDC within 60 days

### D. Reporting and Situational Awareness
- Report all H5N1 suspect and confirmed results to CDC Influenza Division within 2 hours of result finalization
- Participate in weekly Public Health Labs/CDC H5N1 preparedness coordination calls
- Maintain daily reagent and PPE inventory reporting during any CDC-declared elevated H5N1 risk period

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Weekly Readiness Status Reports** — testing capacity, reagent inventory, staff trained
- **Exercise After-Action Reports** — within 60 days of each exercise
- **Agricultural Surveillance Reports** — quarterly, cases tested and outcomes
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance
