# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Public Health Laboratory Workforce Pipeline — Diversification and Retention
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-WORKFORCE2-033  
**Issue Date:** June 2, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)  
**HRSA Award Number:** U68HS030229-02  
**Program Area:** Workforce Development — Laboratory Science Pipeline

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The public health laboratory workforce faces a compounding crisis: the Bureau of Labor Statistics projects a 15% shortage of medical laboratory scientists by 2030, driven by retirements, competition from higher-paying clinical sectors, and decades of underinvestment in laboratory science education pipelines. COVID-19 accelerated this crisis by burning out existing staff while demonstrating the essential role of laboratory scientists in public health response. State public health laboratories that are underpaying, under-recruiting, and under-retaining diverse talent risk cascading capacity failures in future emergencies.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to build sustainable, diverse workforce pipelines through academic partnerships, paid internship programs, retention incentives, and professional development infrastructure aligned with Public Health Labs's National Competency Framework for Public Health Laboratory Practice.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state or large local public health laboratory
- Documented workforce gap analysis showing current or projected FTE shortfalls
- Partnership with at least one accredited clinical laboratory science (CLS), medical laboratory technician (MLT), or public health program (letter required)
- Human resources authority to establish or modify internship and retention programs

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Academic Pipeline Development
- Establish minimum 3 paid practicum or internship positions per year at the laboratory for CLS/MLT students from partnering academic program(s)
- Develop rotational curriculum covering 4+ laboratory sections; document learning objectives aligned with Public Health Labs competency framework
- Commit to providing practicum supervisor training for at least 2 staff members

### B. Diversity Recruitment
- Implement targeted recruitment in partnership with at least one Historically Black College or University (HBCU), Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI), or community college serving underrepresented populations
- Track and report applicant and hire demographics quarterly relative to state population demographics
- Develop laboratory science career awareness program for high school students in Title I schools (minimum 1 event per year)

### C. Retention and Professional Development
- Implement Public Health Labs Public Health Laboratory Professional (PHLP) certification pathway: fund examination and preparation for minimum 5 staff during period of performance
- Conduct stay interviews with all laboratory staff within 6 months of award; develop retention action plans for staff rated at flight-risk
- Implement a formal mentorship program pairing senior staff with early-career laboratory scientists

### D. Succession Planning
- Document current succession plan gaps for critical laboratory functions
- Develop and implement cross-training plan ensuring at least 2 trained staff for each critical function
- Report succession plan status semi-annually

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Quarterly Workforce Reports** — internship positions filled, practicum completions, PHLP certifications, demographic data
- **Retention Action Plan Status** — semi-annual
- **Academic Partnership Activity Reports** — semi-annual
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance including workforce gap reassessment

---

## 6. Budget Requirements

Intern/practicum stipends allowable (competitive with regional clinical laboratory wages). PHLP examination and preparation costs allowable. Recruitment event travel and materials allowable. Staff time for mentorship, curriculum development, and practicum supervision allowable if documented.
