# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Newborn Screening Laboratory Quality Assurance and Panel Expansion
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2022-NBS-018  
**Issue Date:** February 7, 2022  
**Federal Sponsor:** Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)  
**HRSA Award Number:** U22MC33525-01  
**Program Area:** Newborn Screening — Quality Systems

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Newborn screening (NBS) is among the most successful public health prevention programs in history, identifying infants with life-threatening but treatable disorders before symptoms appear. The Recommended Uniform Screening Panel (RUSP) has grown to 35 core and 26 secondary conditions as new disorders become treatable and technologies advance. COVID-19 disrupted NBS programs through staff reductions, courier disruptions, and unprecedented demand for laboratory resources.

This RFP funds state NBS laboratories to strengthen quality management systems, address post-COVID operational gaps, implement RUSP-recommended conditions not yet included in state panels, and improve equity in specimen collection timeliness for preterm and NICU infants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- State-designated NBS laboratory serving as the primary screening laboratory for a U.S. state or territory
- CLIA high-complexity certification for all methods used
- Active participation in CDC NBS Quality Assurance Program and Public Health Labs NBS Technical Assistance
- Commitment of state NBS program coordinator to serve as co-investigator

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Quality Management System Strengthening
- Implement ISO 15189 or CLSI QMS01-compliant quality management system if not already in place
- Achieve passing performance on all CDC NBS QA proficiency panels within period of performance
- Document and remediate at minimum one significant quality gap identified through internal audit within 12 months of award

### B. RUSP Condition Implementation
- Implement testing for at least one RUSP core condition not currently on the state panel (applicant specifies target condition based on state readiness)
- Complete validation, regulatory approval, and live birth screening for target condition within 18 months of award
- Develop or adopt follow-up protocol for newly added condition in collaboration with state follow-up program

### C. NICU and Preterm Infant Specimen Equity
- Analyze current NICU repeat specimen timeliness data; identify hospitals with >10% of NICU specimens received >7 days after collection
- Implement targeted outreach and training to outlier hospitals to achieve <5% late specimens by end of period of performance
- Implement tracking for specimens with documented collection/receipt delays and automate re-collection prompts

### D. Technology Modernization
- Implement or upgrade to a LIMS module supporting electronic results reporting to primary care providers with HL7 messaging
- Achieve electronic result delivery for ≥50% of positive results within 24 hours of finalization

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Semi-Annual Quality Metrics Reports** — QA proficiency scores, turnaround times, NICU timeliness data
- **RUSP Implementation Milestone Reports** — at validation completion and live screening launch
- **Equity Analysis** — annual report with NICU specimen timeliness by hospital
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance
