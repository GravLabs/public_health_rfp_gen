**Issue Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / DHQP  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000583-02  
**Program Area:** Antimicrobial Resistance — Stewardship Support

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Antimicrobial stewardship programs (ASPs) in healthcare facilities depend on timely, high-quality susceptibility data from clinical laboratories. State public health laboratories can support stewardship at scale by providing reference testing, novel resistance confirmation, and population-level AMR surveillance data that no single clinical laboratory can generate. The COVID-19 pandemic exacerbated AMR through increased broad-spectrum antibiotic use, with 2020–2022 data showing significant increases in resistant infections acquired in healthcare settings.

This RFP funds state laboratories to provide rapid reference AMR testing services for healthcare facilities, integrate AMR data into state antibiogram programs, and implement whole-genome sequencing to characterize resistance mechanisms in priority organisms.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with clinical microbiology capability

- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Relationship with state hospital association or health system network for antibiogram aggregation

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Reference AMR Testing Services
- Provide reference minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) testing for isolates referred from healthcare facilities with suspected novel resistance
- Achieve 48-hour turnaround for referred isolates from ARLN-member healthcare facilities
- Accept and process minimum 200 reference isolates per year

### B. State Antibiogram Program
- Aggregate anonymized susceptibility data from at least 20 clinical laboratories across the state to produce an annual state antibiogram
- Produce regional antibiograms stratified by hospital type (community, academic, long-term care)
- Distribute state antibiogram to all contributing facilities and publish on state health department website annually

### C. WGS for Novel Resistance
- Perform WGS on 100% of referred isolates with novel or unusual resistance phenotypes
- Submit WGS data to NCBI and ARLN BioProject within 21 days of sequencing
- Use AMRFinder Plus to characterize resistance gene content and report to referring facility within 5 business days of WGS completion

### D. Stewardship Education
- Conduct at least 4 educational webinars per year for clinical laboratory and pharmacy staff on interpreting AMR laboratory data for stewardship
- Develop and distribute guidance on rapid diagnostic test interpretation for stewardship decision-making

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Annual State Antibiogram** — published by March 31 each year
