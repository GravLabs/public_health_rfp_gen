## 1. Background and Purpose

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) are a class of over 12,000 synthetic chemicals used in industrial processes, food packaging, and firefighting foams. PFAS are persistent in the environment and in human bodies, with exposures linked to thyroid disorders, certain cancers, immune suppression, and developmental effects in children. EPA's finalization of a National Primary Drinking Water Regulation for six PFAS compounds in 2024 created new testing mandates for public water systems. CDC's National Exposure Report and ATSDR PFAS health studies have generated unprecedented demand for PFAS biomonitoring laboratory capacity.

State public health laboratories are uniquely positioned to bridge environmental testing (drinking water, soil, groundwater) and clinical biomonitoring (serum PFAS), providing integrated PFAS exposure assessment for communities near contaminated sites — particularly military installations and industrial facilities.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with LC-MS/MS capability or commitment to acquire
- NELAP accreditation for water testing (or commitment within 12 months)
- CLIA certification for serum/plasma analysis if biomonitoring component included
- Documented presence of PFAS-contaminated sites (military installations, AFFF-impacted areas, industrial facilities) in state
- Partnership with state environmental agency or EPA regional office

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Drinking Water PFAS Testing
- Validate and implement EPA Method 533 or 537.1 for PFAS in drinking water
- Achieve NELAP accreditation for water PFAS methods within 12 months of award
- Provide PFAS testing for public water systems in state at public health rates; prioritize systems serving >10,000 people near known contamination sources

### B. Serum PFAS Biomonitoring
- Implement serum PFAS panel (minimum 25 PFAS per CDC NHANES method) by LC-MS/MS
- Support ATSDR exposure assessment studies for ≥2 contaminated communities during period of performance
- Achieve inter-laboratory precision within 15% CV against CDC reference laboratory

### C. Environmental Site Investigation Support
- Provide soil, groundwater, and surface water PFAS testing for state environmental agency investigations on request
- Develop integrated exposure report linking environmental testing results with biomonitoring findings for affected communities
- Participate in EPA Third Unregulated Contaminant Monitoring Rule (UCMR 5) data reporting

### D. Community Communication Support
- Develop lay-language report templates translating PFAS results for community members
- Partner with state environmental agency to host community results presentations at ≥2 contaminated sites
