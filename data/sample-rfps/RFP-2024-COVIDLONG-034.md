**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000591-PASC  
**Program Area:** COVID-19 — Post-Acute Sequelae (Long COVID)

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Long COVID, or Post-Acute Sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 infection (PASC), affects an estimated 7–23 million Americans with symptoms including fatigue, cognitive impairment, dyspnea, and autonomic dysfunction persisting months to years after acute infection. NIH's RECOVER initiative has enrolled over 17,000 participants but lacks population-level biomarker surveillance infrastructure. State public health laboratories can contribute by implementing standardized Long COVID biomarker testing panels, contributing to the national evidence base, and supporting state-level Long COVID surveillance and clinical trial support networks.

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Long COVID Biomarker Panel Implementation
- Implement standardized biomarker panel including: D-dimer, fibrinogen, CRP, ferritin, anti-nucleocapsid and anti-spike IgG, cytokine panel (IL-6, TNF-α, IFN-γ), and autoantibody screen
- Validate inter-laboratory precision against CDC RECOVER reference laboratory within 15% CV
- Process minimum 50 Long COVID biomarker panels per month from clinical partners

### B. Population-Level Surveillance
- Contribute de-identified biomarker data to CDC RECOVER research database per data sharing agreement
- Implement seroprevalence testing for anti-SARS-CoV-2 antibodies (nucleocapsid + spike) in partnership with at least one population-based surveillance cohort
- Produce quarterly state-level seroprevalence estimates with age and demographic stratification

### C. Clinical Trial Laboratory Support
- Provide GCP-compliant specimen processing and biomarker testing for at least one RECOVER or Long COVID clinical trial at a partnering academic site
- Implement specimen chain-of-custody and aliquoting protocols meeting clinical trial requirements
- Achieve CLIA + CAP accreditation if not already holding CAP (required for trial support)

### D. Standardization and QA
- Participate in Public Health Labs/CDC Long COVID biomarker external quality assurance program when available
- Document all biomarker methods in SOPs reviewed by partnering academic laboratory
