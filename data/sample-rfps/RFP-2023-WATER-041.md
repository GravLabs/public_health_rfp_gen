## Drinking Water Contaminant Surveillance — Nitrates, Microplastics, and Emerging Chemicals
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2023-WATER-041  
**Issue Date:** July 31, 2023  
**Federal Sponsor:** Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) / Office of Groundwater and Drinking Water  
**EPA Award Number:** EPA-OW-OGWDW-2023-G013  
**Program Area:** Environmental Health — Drinking Water Safety

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Safe drinking water is foundational to public health, yet emerging contaminants — including nitrates from agricultural runoff, microplastics, hexavalent chromium, per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS beyond EPA Method 533 analytes), and disinfection byproducts — continue to be identified in community water systems and private wells across the United States. The EPA Safe Drinking Water Act (SDWA) regulatory framework moves slowly compared to the rate of contaminant discovery. State public health laboratories serve as the analytical bridge between regulatory compliance testing and population health protection, providing testing for contaminants not yet regulated or requiring exposure assessment beyond compliance monitoring. This RFP funds expanded state laboratory capacity for drinking water contaminant surveillance beyond PFAS (addressed in RFP-2023-PFAS-025).

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Nitrate and Agricultural Runoff Surveillance
- Expand nitrate and nitrite testing capacity for private wells in agricultural areas: target 500 private well samples/year from targeted vulnerable populations (infants, pregnant women)
- Implement outreach program with county health departments for private well sampling in high-risk agricultural zones
- Report nitrate exceedances (>10 mg/L MCL) within 48 hours to homeowner and local health department

### B. Microplastics Monitoring
- Implement or validate a standardized microplastics detection method for drinking water (ISO 24187 or equivalent) if analytical capability exists or can be established within 12 months
- Conduct baseline microplastics surveillance at ≥10 drinking water sources (surface water and groundwater) and ≥3 finished water treatment plant effluents
- Contribute methods and data to the EPA Microplastics Research Network

### C. Emerging Disinfection Byproducts
- Implement testing for emerging disinfection byproducts including HAAs (haloacetic acids beyond HAA5), haloacetonitriles, and N-nitrosodimethylamine (NDMA) using EPA Method 521 or equivalent
- Screen ≥10 community water systems with known precursor issues (high DOC, bromide) for emerging DBP formation
- Report DBP surveillance data to EPA WaterBase and state drinking water program

### D. Private Well Equity Program
- Establish no-cost or low-cost comprehensive private well testing for uninsured/underinsured residents: nitrates, coliform, arsenic, lead, and pH minimum panel
- Partner with county health departments and community organizations serving rural and tribal communities
- Track testing uptake by geographic area and demographic group quarterly
