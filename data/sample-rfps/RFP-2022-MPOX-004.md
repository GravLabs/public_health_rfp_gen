# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Mpox (Monkeypox) Emergency Response — Diagnostic Testing Capacity
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2022-MPOX-004  
**Issue Date:** August 5, 2022  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / DPDM  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-04  
**Program Area:** Emergency Response — Mpox Outbreak

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

In May 2022, the United States began experiencing an outbreak of mpox (formerly monkeypox) that rapidly expanded to all 50 states. CDC declared a public health emergency and activated emergency response funding to expand diagnostic testing beyond CDC's Poxvirus Laboratory to state and local public health laboratories.

Public Health Labs is distributing CDC emergency supplemental funding to state public health laboratories to rapidly implement orthopoxvirus PCR testing using the CDC-developed assay under Emergency Use Authorization (EUA). This RFP reflects the urgency of the outbreak and uses an expedited review process. The period of performance is intentionally short to match the acute outbreak response window.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- public health member laboratory — state, territorial, or large local public health laboratory
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Access to BSL-2 containment laboratory suitable for orthopoxvirus PCR
- Capability to receive, process, and report lesion swab specimens
- Agreement to report all results to CDC within 24 hours of result availability

Priority consideration will be given to laboratories in jurisdictions with **>50 confirmed mpox cases** at the time of application.

---

## 4. Scope of Work

Given the emergency nature of this funding, all awardees are expected to:

1. **Implement CDC Orthopoxvirus PCR Assay** — validate and implement within 30 days of award using CDC-provided EUA reagents; document validation per CLIA standards
2. **Achieve Testing Volume Targets** — process minimum 20 specimens per week at peak capacity; report capacity weekly to Public Health Labs
3. **Reporting and Surveillance Integration** — submit all results to state epidemiologists and CDC within required timeframes; interface with state surveillance systems; report demographic data per CDC guidance
4. **Specimen Referral Network** — accept specimens from at least 10 healthcare providers or local health departments within the state; provide collection guidance to submitters
5. **Surge Plan** — document surge plan to double testing capacity within 48 hours if outbreak escalates

Awardees are **not** required to conduct contact tracing but must communicate turnaround time expectations to submitters.

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

Due to the emergency nature of this award:
- **Weekly Operational Reports** — testing volumes, turnaround time, reagent inventory — due every Monday via Public Health Labs Emergency Portal
- **Bi-Monthly Progress Reports** — narrative progress updates at 60 and 120 days
- **Final Report** — 45 days post-period of performance
- **Reagent Shortfall Alerts** — within 24 hours of identifying a supply constraint that could impact testing

---

## 6. Budget Requirements

Allowable: PCR reagents (including CDC EUA materials supplement), specimen collection supplies, personnel (overtime allowable during surge), shipping and handling for specimens, biosafety equipment, result reporting system modifications.

Equipment allowable only if essential for assay implementation and not available through other means. Capital equipment over $5,000 requires Public Health Labs pre-approval.
