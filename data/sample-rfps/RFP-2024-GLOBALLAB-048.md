**Program Area:** Global Health Security — Laboratory Systems

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated that pathogen threats do not respect borders — laboratory diagnostic gaps in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs) allowed SARS-CoV-2 to spread undetected for weeks. The International Health Regulations (IHR 2005) require that all WHO Member States develop core public health capacities including laboratory capacity. GHSA (Global Health Security Agenda) Action Package 3 (Laboratory Systems) provides the framework, and U.S. state public health laboratories with deep technical expertise are uniquely positioned to serve as institutional partners to LMICs for laboratory systems strengthening. This RFP funds U.S. state public health laboratories to partner with a national reference laboratory in an Public Health Labs-identified priority country to build sustainable public health laboratory capacity.

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. LMIC Partner Laboratory Assessment
- Conduct SLIPTA (Stepwise Laboratory Improvement Process Towards Accreditation) baseline audit at partner national reference laboratory within 6 months of award
- Identify top 5 laboratory systems gaps: quality management, specimen referral, biosafety, equipment, workforce
- Develop 3-year capacity building work plan jointly with partner laboratory

### B. Technical Assistance and Training
- Provide minimum 3 in-country technical assistance visits per year (on-site)
- Deliver training in quality management systems (QMS) covering: document control, CAPA, internal audit, and SLIPTA criteria
- Support partner laboratory in training a "train-the-trainer" cadre for at least 5 provincial laboratory supervisors

### C. Biosafety and Infrastructure
- Assess and remediate critical biosafety gaps (BSL-2 minimum): sharps disposal, spill protocols, PPE availability, waste decontamination
- Support procurement and commissioning of priority equipment (biosafety cabinets, centrifuges, autoclave validation)
- Do not fund single-vendor capital equipment procurement >$50,000 without Public Health Labs prior approval

### D. Accreditation Pathway
- Support partner laboratory in progressing from SLIPTA 1-star to ≥3-star within the 36-month period of performance
- Prepare partner for WHO-AFRO SLIPTA external audit at end of year 3
- Document all training, SOPs, and QMS records in partner-owned document management system
