**Program Area:** Foodborne Disease — PulseNet Laboratory Network

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

PulseNet, CDC's national molecular subtyping network for foodborne disease surveillance, transitioned from pulsed-field gel electrophoresis (PFGE) to whole-genome sequencing (WGS) as the national standard. WGS provides dramatically higher resolution for cluster detection and outbreak investigation for Salmonella, STEC O157 and non-O157, Listeria monocytogenes, Campylobacter, and Vibrio. State PulseNet laboratories are the frontline of this system and must sustain WGS throughput, data quality, and turnaround to enable timely national outbreak detection.

This RFP funds state PulseNet laboratories to sustain and enhance their foodborne WGS capacity, improve data completeness, and integrate with FDA's GenomeTrakr network for comprehensive food safety genomic surveillance.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current PulseNet member laboratory in good standing
- Active Illumina WGS platform or equivalent validated for PulseNet submissions
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Agreement to submit all isolates to NCBI via BioProject within 4 weeks of sequencing
- Passing score on most recent PulseNet WGS proficiency panel

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. WGS Throughput and Coverage
- Achieve ≥90% sequencing of all reportable Salmonella, STEC, and Listeria monocytogenes isolates within 4 weeks of isolation
- Implement batch sequencing workflow to process minimum 48 isolates per run
- Submit all sequences to CDC BioProject and NCBI SRA within required timeframes

### B. Data Quality and Bioinformatics
- Implement CDC-recommended bioinformatics pipeline (CFSAN SNP Pipeline or equivalent) for all submissions
- Achieve Phred quality scores ≥Q30 on ≥80% of reads for all submitted sequences
- Participate in PulseNet annual WGS quality assurance exercise

### C. Outbreak Investigation Response
- Provide WGS results for outbreak isolates within 5 business days of request from state or CDC epidemiology
- Designate 24/7 on-call laboratory contact for PulseNet outbreak activations
- Contribute to at least one multi-state outbreak investigation per year

### D. GenomeTrakr Integration
- Submit Listeria and Salmonella environmental isolates from food safety partners to GenomeTrakr BioProject
- Develop formal agreement with state agriculture department for environmental isolate submission pipeline

## 5. Reporting Requirements
