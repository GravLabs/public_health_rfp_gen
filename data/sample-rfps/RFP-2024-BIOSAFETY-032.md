# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## BSL-3 Infrastructure Modernization — Post-COVID Recommissioning and Enhancement
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-BIOSAFETY-032  
**Issue Date:** March 11, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / DSAT  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-BSL-01  
**Program Area:** Biosafety — Laboratory Infrastructure

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

BSL-3 laboratories capable of handling Biosafety Level 3 pathogens (Mycobacterium tuberculosis, SARS-CoV-2 live virus work, Coxiella burnetii, select agents, and others) form a critical tier of the national laboratory security architecture. COVID-19 revealed that several state BSL-3 laboratories had not been actively maintained, with deferred certifications, aging HVAC systems, and workforce attrition undermining capacity precisely when it was needed most.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to recommission, upgrade, or certify BSL-3 laboratory space, bring staff back to current biosafety training requirements, and document sustainable maintenance protocols to prevent future lapse.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with existing BSL-3 laboratory space requiring recommissioning, certification lapse remediation, or significant infrastructure upgrade
- Documentation of BSL-3 certification gap, deferred maintenance, or current certification status
- Commitment of laboratory director and institutional biosafety officer (IBO) as co-investigators
- Letter from institution's Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBC) confirming scope of proposed work

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Physical Infrastructure Upgrade or Recommissioning
- Address documented BSL-3 deficiencies: HVAC directional airflow, negative pressure verification, autoclave certification, airlock integrity, HEPA filtration
- Achieve NSF/ANSI 49 certification for all Class II Type B2 biosafety cabinets used in BSL-3 operations
- Document and pass CDC/Public Health Labs BSL-3 infrastructure checklist within 18 months of award

### B. Workforce Biosafety Training
- Achieve current biosafety training certification (Select Agent regulations, respiratory protection, emergency response) for all staff who will work in BSL-3 space
- Conduct and document annual BSL-3 emergency drills (spill, personnel exposure, power failure)
- Designate a trained backup IBO with documented succession plan

### C. Sustainable Maintenance Protocol
- Develop a 5-year BSL-3 maintenance schedule with defined inspection intervals, responsible parties, and funding sources
- Implement digital BSL-3 maintenance log with Public Health Labs-template compliance documentation
- Conduct formal annual BSL-3 operations review with IBC

### D. Capacity Activation
- Following recommissioning, demonstrate operational BSL-3 capacity by performing ≥50 BSL-3 procedures during the period of performance (e.g., TB culture, SARS-CoV-2 live virus manipulation for QC, or approved Select Agent work)
- Document restored testing capacity and report to Public Health Labs

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Infrastructure Milestone Reports** — at 6, 12, 18 months: documenting completed deficiency remediation
- **IBC Annual Review Submission** — copy to Public Health Labs
- **BSL-3 Certification Documentation** — within 30 days of achieving certification/recertification
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance with 5-year maintenance plan attached

---

## 6. Budget Requirements

HVAC repair and certification, BSC certification, autoclave validation, physical security upgrades, and training are primary allowable costs. Construction costs are allowable only for BSL-3 infrastructure repairs (not new construction or non-BSL areas). Equipment for BSL-3 operations (centrifuge rotors, biosafety equipment) allowable.
