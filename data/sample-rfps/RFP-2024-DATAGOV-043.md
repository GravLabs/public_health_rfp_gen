## Laboratory Data Governance and Quality Systems — LIMS Modernization and Interoperability
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-DATAGOV-043  
**Issue Date:** April 22, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / OID / DPHCID  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000599-DG  
**Program Area:** Laboratory Data Systems — Governance and Interoperability

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Public health laboratory data systems are fragmented, aging, and inconsistently interoperable. COVID-19 exposed critical gaps: laboratory information management systems (LIMS) could not handle surge volumes, result reporting to health departments was delayed or manual, and data quality for public health decision-making was poor. CDC's Data Modernization Initiative (DMI) and the HL7 FHIR R4 electronic laboratory reporting (ELR) mandate create a window to modernize public health laboratory data infrastructure. This RFP funds state public health laboratories to implement or upgrade LIMS, establish data governance frameworks, and achieve certified HL7 FHIR R4-compliant electronic laboratory reporting for all notifiable conditions.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state public health laboratory
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Documented LIMS modernization need (system >8 years old, manual reporting workflows, or lack of HL7 ELR capability for ≥5 notifiable conditions)
- State health department IT and epidemiology program committed to integration

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. LIMS Modernization or Upgrade
- Implement or upgrade LIMS to a cloud-capable, multi-platform system supporting all major testing sections (virology, bacteriology, chemistry, serology)
- Achieve LIMS uptime ≥99.5% measured monthly; document disaster recovery plan with ≤4 hour RTO (recovery time objective)
- Implement LIMS-based quality management system (QMS) covering: corrective action (CAPA), document control, instrument maintenance, and proficiency testing tracking

### B. HL7 FHIR R4 Electronic Laboratory Reporting
- Achieve certified HL7 FHIR R4-compliant ELR for all CDC nationally notifiable conditions reportable from the laboratory within 24 months of award
- Participate in CDC AIMS Platform FHIR onboarding program and complete all validation milestones
- Implement real-time result transmission for priority conditions (influenza, SARS-CoV-2, Salmonella, N. meningitidis) with ≤24-hour reporting latency from result finalization

### C. Data Governance Framework
- Develop and implement a laboratory data governance policy covering: data ownership, access controls, data dictionary, result amendment protocols, and retention schedules
- Implement role-based access control (RBAC) in LIMS with audit trails for all data access and modification events
- Complete SOC 2 Type I assessment or equivalent for LIMS data security posture within 24 months

### D. Interoperability and Analytics
- Implement bi-directional interface with state epidemiology surveillance system (e.g., Maven, ESSENCE, RHINO) for automated case investigation support
- Develop laboratory analytics dashboard for leadership showing: daily test volumes, turnaround times, positivity rates, and quality metrics
- Contribute LIMS implementation learnings to Public Health Labs LIMS Community of Practice
