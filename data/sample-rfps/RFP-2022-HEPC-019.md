## Hepatitis C Elimination — Laboratory Capacity for Reflex Testing and Linkage to Care
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2022-HEPC-019  
**Issue Date:** June 20, 2022  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / DVHAP  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU65PS004658-01  
**Program Area:** Viral Hepatitis — Hepatitis C Elimination

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection affects an estimated 2.4 million Americans and is now curable with direct-acting antivirals (DAAs) achieving >95% sustained virologic response. The United States has set a national goal to eliminate HCV as a public health threat by 2030. A critical barrier to elimination is the testing-to-treatment gap: many individuals who test HCV antibody positive are never confirmed by RNA testing and never linked to treatment.

State public health laboratories can play a transformative role by offering reflex HCV RNA testing (automatic RNA testing following a positive antibody) and integrating genotyping to guide treatment selection. This RFP funds laboratories to implement or expand reflex HCV RNA testing at no cost to patients, with priority on populations most affected by HCV — persons who inject drugs, individuals experiencing homelessness, and incarcerated populations.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state or large local public health laboratory with HCV antibody testing capability
- CLIA certification for molecular testing (HCV RNA quantification)
- Partnership with state hepatitis C elimination program (letter of support required)
- Commitment to provide reflex RNA testing at no cost to uninsured patients

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Reflex HCV RNA Implementation
- Implement automatic reflex HCV RNA testing for all reactive HCV antibody results (signal-to-cutoff ≥3.8 or equivalent)
- Achieve turnaround time of ≤3 business days from antibody reactive result to RNA result available
- Process minimum 500 reflex RNA tests per month at peak capacity

### B. Priority Population Access
- Establish specimen submission agreements with at least 3 syringe services programs (SSPs), correctional health programs, or homeless services providers
- Provide HCV RNA testing at no cost for specimens from uninsured patients from priority population partners
- Report testing volumes from priority populations quarterly

### C. Genotyping and Treatment Linkage
- Offer HCV genotyping for RNA-positive cases to support treatment planning (pan-genotypic DAAs reduce need, but genotyping supports complex cases)
- Partner with state hepatitis program to ensure linkage-to-care data is captured for patients with confirmed active HCV infection
- Achieve ≥80% of confirmed HCV-positive patients referred to treatment within 30 days

### D. Data Integration
- Submit HCV laboratory data to state surveillance system within 24 hours of finalization
- Participate in CDC NHBS (National HIV Behavioral Surveillance) testing linkage if state is a participating site

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Linkage-to-Care Reports** — semi-annual, proportion linked within 30 days
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance
