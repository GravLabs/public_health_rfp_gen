# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Bioinformatics Workforce Development — Next-Generation Sequencing Analysis Capacity
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2022-BIOIN-005b  
**Issue Date:** October 14, 2022  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / CSELS  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000591-02  
**Program Area:** Workforce Development — Bioinformatics

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The rapid adoption of next-generation sequencing (NGS) during COVID-19 revealed a critical gap: public health laboratories had sequencing instruments but lacked trained bioinformaticians to analyze the data. CDC and Public Health Labs identified bioinformatics workforce development as a tier-1 priority for the post-pandemic laboratory system.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to build sustainable bioinformatics capacity — through hiring, training, and academic partnership — to support not only SARS-CoV-2 variant surveillance but the full range of pathogen NGS use cases including foodborne illness (WGS for outbreak investigation), tuberculosis whole-genome sequencing, and antimicrobial resistance (AMR) surveillance.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state or large local public health laboratory
- Demonstrated NGS capability (at least one sequencing platform operational)
- Commitment of laboratory director to support bioinformatics position(s) beyond the award period
- Academic partnership letter of intent (formal agreement not required at application, but required within 90 days of award)

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Workforce Expansion
- Hire or fund at minimum 0.5 FTE bioinformatician (new or expanded position) dedicated to public health genomics
- Establish a clear job description and competency framework aligned with Public Health Labs/CDC bioinformatics competency model
- Document a sustainability plan for the position beyond the period of performance

### B. Training and Skill Building
- All laboratory bioinformatics staff complete at minimum one Public Health Labs-approved bioinformatics training module within 6 months of award
- Laboratory participates in at least two Public Health Labs bioinformatics community of practice webinars per year
- Bioinformatician(s) complete proficiency assessment on Galaxy or command-line bioinformatics workflows within 12 months

### C. Pipeline Development
- Implement validated bioinformatics pipelines for at least two of: SARS-CoV-2 variant calling, foodborne pathogen WGS, TB WGS, AMR gene detection
- Document all pipelines in standard operating procedures (SOPs) maintained in laboratory document control system
- Share pipeline code or documentation with Public Health Labs member community via Public Health Labs GitHub or equivalent

### D. Academic Partnership
- Formalize partnership with at least one regional college or university for: student practicums, curriculum alignment, or workforce pipeline development
- Co-develop at least one training module or case study with academic partner for use in public health bioinformatics education

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements
