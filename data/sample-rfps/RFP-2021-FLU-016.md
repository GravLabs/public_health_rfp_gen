**Program Area:** Influenza — Sentinel Surveillance

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Seasonal influenza surveillance through the U.S. Influenza Sentinel Provider Surveillance Network (ILINet) and state public health laboratories forms the backbone of national influenza situational awareness. COVID-19 demonstrated that respiratory pathogen surveillance must be more agile and broader than existing infrastructure — sentinel networks designed for seasonal influenza proved insufficient to simultaneously monitor SARS-CoV-2, RSV, and influenza during co-circulation seasons. This RFP funds state laboratories to strengthen influenza surveillance infrastructure for the post-COVID era, including concurrent respiratory virus monitoring and enhanced severe disease surveillance in ICU settings.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with rRT-PCR influenza A/B typing and subtyping capability
- Active ILINet and FluView Interactive reporting participant
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Existing sentinel provider network with minimum 10 participating providers

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Multiplex Respiratory Surveillance
- Implement or expand multiplexed respiratory panel testing (influenza A/B, SARS-CoV-2, RSV minimum) at state laboratory level
- Expand ILINet sentinel provider network by minimum 5 providers, prioritizing emergency departments and urgent care
- Achieve ≥95% of sentinel specimens tested within 3 business days of receipt

### B. Severe Disease Surveillance
- Establish ICU-based severe acute respiratory illness (SARI) surveillance in partnership with ≥2 academic medical centers
- Implement systematic specimen collection from SARI patients with unknown etiology
- Contribute SARI data to CDC Influenza Hospitalization Surveillance Network (FluSurv-NET)

### C. Antigenic and Genetic Characterization
- Submit ≥15% of influenza A(H3N2) and A(H1N1)pdm09 positive specimens to CDC for antigenic and genetic characterization
- Submit complete genome sequences for all novel influenza A subtypes (H5, H7, H9) to GenBank within 14 days
- Participate in WHO Global Influenza Surveillance and Response System (GISRS) through CDC

### D. Rapid Response Capacity
- Maintain capability to activate 24/7 influenza testing within 4 hours of a declared outbreak alert
- Stockpile minimum 500 influenza rRT-PCR reactions for emergency surge
