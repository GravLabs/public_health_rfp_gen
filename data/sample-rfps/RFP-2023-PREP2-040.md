**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / DHAP  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU62PS005196-02  
**Program Area:** HIV Prevention — PrEP Access and Monitoring

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) with emtricitabine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (TDF/FTC) or emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide (TAF/FTC) reduces HIV acquisition by >99% when taken as prescribed, yet only approximately 30% of the 1.2 million Americans who would benefit from PrEP are currently using it. A critical barrier is access to laboratory monitoring required by clinical guidelines: HIV testing every 3 months, renal function (creatinine, eGFR) every 6 months, and STI testing every 3–6 months. In underserved communities and at community-based organizations (CBOs) providing PrEP navigation, laboratory access is a structural barrier to PrEP uptake and persistence. This RFP funds state public health laboratories to expand PrEP monitoring laboratory capacity in high-need settings.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with HIV serology and chemistry testing capability
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Partnership with at least 2 community-based PrEP providers or sexual health clinics serving high-burden populations
- Jurisdiction with ≥500 new HIV diagnoses/year OR Ending the HIV Epidemic (EHE) priority jurisdiction

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. PrEP Monitoring Laboratory Package
- Implement bundled PrEP monitoring laboratory panel: 4th-generation HIV Ag/Ab combination assay, creatinine/eGFR, gonorrhea and chlamydia NAAT (pharyngeal, rectal, urogenital), syphilis serology (treponemal + RPR)
- Process PrEP monitoring panels with 5-business-day turnaround; 2-business-day for priority flagged
- Implement specimen self-collection program: validate home specimen collection kits for HIV, STI, and urine (renal function) submitted by mail

### B. Pharmacy and CBO Laboratory Partnership
- Develop laboratory service agreements with ≥5 PrEP-prescribing pharmacies or CBOs in EHE priority areas, enabling specimen drop-off and electronic result delivery
- Train CBO staff in specimen collection and transport requirements for each assay in PrEP monitoring panel
- Implement telehealth-compatible result reporting with lab director consultation availability for CBO clients without primary care

### C. Equity-Centered Access
- Track PrEP monitoring utilization by race/ethnicity, age group, and geographic area (urban/rural)
- Report quarterly disparities data to state HIV program and identify coverage gaps
- Pilot reduced-cost or zero-cost PrEP monitoring for uninsured clients in coordination with state 340B program

### D. Data Integration
- Implement bi-directional electronic reporting with state HIV surveillance system for all new HIV diagnoses identified through PrEP monitoring
- Report PrEP monitoring volumes and positivity rates to CDC DHAP quarterly
