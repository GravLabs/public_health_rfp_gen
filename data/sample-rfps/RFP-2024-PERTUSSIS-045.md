## 1. Background and Purpose

Pertussis (whooping cough) resurged significantly in the United States in 2024, with case counts on track to exceed 30,000 — the highest in over a decade — driven by waning immunity from acellular pertussis vaccines, increased detection from molecular methods, and school-age outbreak clusters. Bordetella pertussis strain evolution, including the spread of prn-deficient (pertactin-negative) strains that may evade vaccine immunity, requires laboratory-based genomic characterization to track vaccine-driven selection pressure. State laboratories serve as the primary confirmatory testing resource for pertussis outbreak investigation and must support both diagnostic surge and genomic epidemiology.

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Rapid Diagnostic Capacity
- Maintain PCR-based Bordetella pertussis and parapertussis testing with 48-hour turnaround under routine conditions and 24-hour during outbreak declaration
- Implement or validate multiplex PCR capable of simultaneously detecting B. pertussis, B. parapertussis, and B. holmesii from nasopharyngeal specimens
- Support culture confirmation of PCR-positive specimens at minimum 10% sampling rate for genomic characterization

### B. Molecular Epidemiology and WGS
- Implement WGS for outbreak cluster confirmation: sequence all culture-positive pertussis specimens from outbreak investigations
- Type isolates for pertactin gene status (prn intact vs prn-deficient) and fim2/fim3 fimbriae type for all sequenced strains
- Submit sequences to CDC BioProject and NCBI GenBank within 21 days of sequencing completion

### C. Vaccine Effectiveness Support
- Partner with state immunization program to collect vaccination history for PCR-positive pertussis cases
- Report vaccine status data (DTaP doses, Tdap timing) to CDC pertussis surveillance system for vaccine effectiveness analysis
- Investigate outbreak clusters for evidence of vaccine-driven strain selection

### D. Outbreak Communication
- Implement 24-hour notification protocol for cluster detection (≥2 linked cases within 21 days)
- Provide laboratory data for outbreak line lists and epidemiological reports within 48 hours of state epi request
