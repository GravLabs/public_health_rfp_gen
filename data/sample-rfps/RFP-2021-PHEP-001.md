**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-01  
**Program Area:** Emergency Preparedness and Response

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The Association of Public Health Laboratories (Public Health Labs) has received Cooperative Agreement funding from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) under the Public Health Emergency Preparedness (PHEP) program to enhance state and local public health laboratory capacity for emergency detection and response. This RFP solicits proposals from public health member laboratories to strengthen foundational laboratory capabilities that underpin emergency response readiness.

The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated significant gaps in state public health laboratory surge capacity, information systems interoperability, and workforce preparedness. PHEP funding in this cycle prioritizes closing those gaps across the Laboratory Response Network (LRN) and broader public health laboratory ecosystem.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for funding under this RFP, applicants must:

- Be a current public health member laboratory in good standing
- Operate as a state, territorial, local, or tribal (STLT) public health laboratory
- Hold current Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA) certification appropriate to proposed work
- Demonstrate participation in Public Health Labs's Laboratory Emergency Response Network activities
- Not have unresolved audit findings related to prior federal awards
- Comply with all federal civil rights requirements (45 CFR Part 80)

Laboratories affiliated with academic medical centers or hospital systems are **not eligible** unless they serve as the designated state public health laboratory.

## 4. Priority Areas and Scope of Work

Applicants must select **at least two** of the following priority areas and describe measurable activities for each:

### Priority Area A: Surge Testing Capacity
- Procure or validate high-throughput testing platforms for respiratory pathogen panels
- Establish mutual aid agreements with neighboring state laboratories for surge overflow testing
- Document validation of at least one new Emergency Use Authorization (EUA) assay

### Priority Area B: Information Systems Modernization
- Implement or upgrade Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS) to support HL7 FHIR-compliant electronic laboratory reporting (ELR)
- Achieve 95%+ timely electronic reporting to CDC BioSense Platform within the period of performance
- Participate in CDC's Data Modernization Initiative (DMI) pilot if selected

### Priority Area C: Workforce Development and Cross-Training
- Develop and implement cross-training curricula for laboratory staff to work across multiple test modalities during surge events
- Achieve minimum 80% of laboratory staff completing the Public Health Labs Emergency Preparedness and Response online training series
- Document succession plans for at least three critical laboratory leadership positions

### Priority Area D: Supply Chain Resilience
- Establish and maintain a minimum 90-day stockpile of critical reagents and consumables for Tier 1 LRN assays
- Develop and test at least two alternative supplier relationships for critical reagents
- Conduct and document a supply chain vulnerability assessment

## 5. Reporting Requirements

Recipients must submit:

- **Quarterly Progress Reports** — due 30 days after each quarter end; use Public Health Labs's standard reporting template
- **Financial Status Reports** — quarterly via Payment Management System (PMS)
- **Final Performance Report** — due 90 days after period of performance end
- **Data Submissions** — all required CDC data elements submitted via established CDC channels on the schedule specified in the Notice of Award

Failure to submit reports on time may result in withholding of future payments.

---

## 6. Budget Requirements

Allowable costs include:
- Personnel (salary + fringe, must be proportional to effort)
- Equipment (items ≥ $5,000 per unit require prior approval if not in approved budget)
- Supplies and reagents
- Training and travel (domestic only; prior approval required for international travel)
- Subcontracts (require Public Health Labs approval before execution)
- Indirect costs (at negotiated rate)

Unallowable costs include:
- Construction or renovation
- Lobbying activities
- Entertainment
- Fundraising

Budgets must include a justification narrative. Period of performance extensions will not be granted except in extraordinary circumstances.

---

## 7. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be scored on a 100-point scale:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Technical Merit — clarity of objectives and activities | 30 |
| Feasibility — realistic timeline, staff capacity | 25 |
| Impact — measurable outcomes tied to PHEP metrics | 20 |
| Organizational Capacity — prior performance, CLIA status | 15 |
| Budget Justification — cost-effectiveness | 10 |

A minimum score of 70 is required for funding consideration.
