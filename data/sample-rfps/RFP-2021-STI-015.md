---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Neisseria gonorrhoeae has demonstrated resistance to every antibiotic class recommended for treatment over its history. Ceftriaxone, the last remaining recommended single-agent therapy in the United States, now faces documented reduced susceptibility and confirmed treatment failures internationally. The Gonococcal Isolate Surveillance Project (GISP) and its successor Strengthening the U.S. Response to Resistant Gonorrhea (SURRG) depend on state public health laboratory capacity to culture, perform antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST), and conduct WGS on gonorrhea isolates from sentinel sites.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to maintain and expand GISP/SURRG participation, implement WGS for gonococcal isolates, and support state STI programs with laboratory diagnostics.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current GISP or SURRG participating laboratory
- Capability to perform N. gonorrhoeae culture and Etest or agar dilution MIC testing for at minimum ceftriaxone, azithromycin, and ciprofloxacin
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Agreement to submit all isolates and AST data to CDC DSTDP within specified timelines

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. GISP/SURRG Participation
- Process minimum 30 urethral/cervical N. gonorrhoeae isolates per month from sentinel STI clinics
- Perform MIC testing for ceftriaxone, azithromycin, ciprofloxacin, tetracycline, and penicillin using Etest per CDC protocol
- Submit isolates and AST results to CDC by the 15th of each month

### B. WGS for Resistance Characterization
- Implement WGS for all isolates with elevated ceftriaxone MIC (≥0.125 μg/mL) or azithromycin MIC (≥2.0 μg/mL)
- Submit WGS data to NCBI BioProject within 30 days of sequencing
- Analyze resistance determinants using NGMASTER or equivalent typing tool

### C. State STI Laboratory Support
- Provide culture-based gonorrhea confirmatory testing for state STI program on request
- Conduct annual quality assurance review with sentinel clinic staff for specimen collection and transport

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Monthly GISP Data Submissions** — isolate counts, MIC distributions, per CDC protocol
- **Quarterly Progress Reports** — WGS volumes, elevated MIC events
- **Treatment Failure Reports** — within 24 hours of suspected treatment failure case identified
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance
