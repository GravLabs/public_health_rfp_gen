# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Mpox Genomic Surveillance — Clade Ib Detection and Clinical Diagnostic Expansion
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-MPOX-044  
**Issue Date:** November 18, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / NCIRD / Poxvirus Branch  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000601-MPOX  
**Program Area:** Emerging Infectious Disease — Mpox (Clade Ib)

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The 2022 global mpox outbreak caused by clade IIb was declared a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC) and resulted in over 30,000 U.S. cases. A new clade Ib variant emerged in the Democratic Republic of Congo in 2023–2024 with higher transmissibility and case fatality rates, prompting a second WHO PHEIC in August 2024. State public health laboratories must maintain mpox diagnostic capacity, implement clade-discriminating assays to distinguish IIb from Ib, and contribute genomic surveillance data to detect Ib introduction. This RFP builds on the 2022 emergency infrastructure by funding sustained clade-discriminating mpox surveillance capability at state laboratories.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with existing orthopoxvirus or mpox PCR capability (CDC-validated LRN-B or LRN-B reference lab)
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Demonstrated mpox testing capacity from 2022 outbreak (minimum 50 specimens tested)

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Clade-Discriminating Assay Implementation
- Implement CDC-validated clade Ib discriminating real-time PCR assay within 60 days of award
- Achieve clade discrimination for all mpox-positive specimens: IIb vs Ib assignment with documented sensitivity ≥99%
- Report clade assignments to CDC Poxvirus Branch within 24 hours of positive result

### B. Clinical Diagnostic Surge Maintenance
- Maintain mpox PCR testing capacity of ≥50 specimens/day during surge activation
- Implement after-hours testing capability with ≤4 hour notification to on-call staff for priority specimens
- Achieve 24-hour turnaround for all submitted mpox specimens during non-surge operations

### C. Whole Genome Sequencing
- Sequence minimum 15% of clade Ib-positive specimens using Illumina or ONT-based WGS approach
- Submit clade Ib sequences to NCBI GenBank and CDC PoxView within 14 days of sequencing
- Participate in CDC mpox genomic surveillance working group for cluster detection and outbreak response

### D. Surveillance Integration
- Implement automated reporting of mpox positives to state sexually transmitted infection (STI) surveillance program and CDC SAMS portal
- Track demographic distribution (age, sex, vaccination status, HIV status) for all mpox-positive specimens in coordination with state epidemiology
