---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Tuberculosis (TB) remains the leading infectious disease killer worldwide and a persistent public health challenge in the United States. Whole-genome sequencing (WGS) of Mycobacterium tuberculosis has transformed TB outbreak investigation and drug resistance detection, enabling molecular cluster identification with resolution impossible with traditional RFLP or MIRU-VNTR typing. CDC's Division of Tuberculosis Elimination (DTBE) has established WGS as the standard for TB genotyping and is funding state public health laboratories to build independent WGS capacity rather than relying entirely on CDC's centralized laboratory.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state public health laboratory performing Mycobacterium tuberculosis culture and drug susceptibility testing
- BSL-3 containment for culture; BSL-2 acceptable for DNA extraction from inactivated isolates
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Agreement to submit all TB WGS data to CDC's National TB Genotyping Service (NTBGS) in FASTQ format within specified timelines
- Priority for laboratories in high-TB-burden states (>50 cases annually)

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. WGS Implementation and Validation
- Validate complete WGS workflow: DNA extraction from inactivated M. tuberculosis → library preparation → Illumina sequencing → bioinformatics (TB-Profiler or Clockwork) → NTBGS submission
- Achieve turnaround time of ≤30 days from culture confirmation to WGS results available to state TB program
- Process minimum 80% of culture-confirmed TB cases in the state within period of performance

### B. Drug Resistance Detection Integration
- Use WGS results to confirm or predict drug susceptibility for at least rifampin, isoniazid, fluoroquinolones, and aminoglycosides
- Integrate molecular drug resistance predictions with culture-based DST results in laboratory reporting
- Report discordant WGS/culture DST results to CDC within 5 business days

### C. Outbreak Investigation Support
- Provide WGS-based cluster analysis within 5 business days of receiving a request from state TB program for outbreak investigation support
- Develop and test protocol for emergency WGS turnaround (<7 days) for suspected active outbreak
- Participate in CDC NTBGS data validation exercises
