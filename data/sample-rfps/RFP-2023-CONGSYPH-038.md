---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Congenital syphilis (CS) cases in the United States reached 3,761 in 2022 — a 755% increase from 2012 and the highest number recorded since 1991. CS causes stillbirth, neonatal death, and long-term neurological, skeletal, and sensory disabilities. The epidemic is driven by increasing primary and secondary syphilis among women of reproductive age, combined with failures in prenatal testing, treatment access, and partner services. State public health laboratories can directly impact CS rates by expanding syphilis testing capacity, implementing algorithm-compliant reflex testing, and supporting point-of-care testing in high-burden prenatal and obstetric settings.

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Syphilis Testing Algorithm Implementation
- Implement CDC/Public Health Labs-recommended reverse sequence syphilis algorithm: treponemal EIA/CIA first, reflex to RPR, reflex to second treponemal test (TPPA or TP-PA) for discordant results
- Document full algorithm in CLIA-validated SOP
- Achieve turnaround for reflex testing: RPR ≤24 hours from treponemal reactive, confirmatory ≤72 hours

### B. Surge Capacity Expansion
- Demonstrate capacity to process 200% of current syphilis testing volume without additional instrumentation, through optimized workflow and after-hours protocols
- Establish on-call testing protocol for urgent prenatal syphilis testing (labor and delivery unit requests) with ≤2 hour turnaround
- Implement bi-directional electronic result reporting with at least one major EHR system in the jurisdiction

### C. Point-of-Care Testing Support
- Validate and support rollout of CLIA-waived syphilis point-of-care tests (e.g., Chembio DPP Syphilis Screen) at ≥3 prenatal/OB-GYN or community clinic sites in high-CS-burden areas
- Provide laboratory consultation, QA oversight, and reactive specimen confirmatory testing for POC site network
- Track POC sensitivity/specificity vs. laboratory serology for jurisdiction-specific QA

### D. Newborn CS Diagnostic Support
- Ensure laboratory capability for non-treponemal testing (RPR) on neonatal serum within 24 hours of birth for infants of seropositive mothers
- Implement VDRL testing on CSF specimens for neurosyphilis evaluation (infants and adults) with appropriate laboratory QA controls
