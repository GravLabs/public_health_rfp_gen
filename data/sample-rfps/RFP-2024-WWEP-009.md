# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Wastewater Epidemiology Program — Pathogen Expansion Beyond SARS-CoV-2
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-WWEP-009  
**Issue Date:** January 22, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / CSELS  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000591-03  
**Program Area:** Wastewater Surveillance — Pathogen Expansion

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The National Wastewater Surveillance System (NWSS), established during COVID-19, demonstrated that wastewater epidemiology provides a leading indicator of community disease burden that complements clinical surveillance. CDC has invested in expanding NWSS beyond SARS-CoV-2 to a broader panel of priority pathogens, including influenza, RSV, mpox, norovirus, and antimicrobial-resistant organisms (AROs).

State public health laboratories that serve as NWSS laboratories are positioned to be the technical backbone of this expansion. This RFP funds laboratory capacity, method validation, and data infrastructure to support multi-pathogen wastewater surveillance at the state level, with a focus on sentinel sites covering populations underrepresented in clinical surveillance.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current NWSS participating laboratory OR committed to NWSS enrollment within 60 days of award
- Digital PCR (dPCR) or real-time qPCR capability for wastewater matrices
- CLIA certification (wastewater testing is research-exempt but CLIA-certified workflows preferred)
- Agreement to submit all data to CDC NWSS portal using Public Health Labs-prescribed format
- Access to minimum 5 wastewater treatment plant sampling sites within state

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Multi-Pathogen Method Validation
- Validate quantitative detection methods for at least 4 additional pathogens beyond SARS-CoV-2 from the CDC NWSS priority list (influenza A/B, RSV, norovirus GI/GII, mpox)
- Implement digital PCR for at least 2 pathogens to improve sensitivity at low prevalence
- Achieve inter-laboratory comparability with at least 2 other NWSS laboratories via proficiency samples

### B. Sentinel Site Expansion
- Expand wastewater sampling to at least 3 new sentinel sites specifically covering underserved populations (correctional facilities, unhoused populations, low-income census tracts)
- Develop sampling and logistics protocols for non-standard collection points (e.g., manholes, pump stations)
- Achieve weekly sampling frequency at all sentinel sites

### C. Data Quality and Automation
- Implement automated data processing pipeline from raw Ct values to normalized concentration per copy/ml
- Achieve data submission to CDC NWSS within 48 hours of sample analysis completion
- Implement quality control metrics: positive control pass rate ≥95%, inhibition check on all samples

### D. Epidemiological Integration
- Partner with state epidemiology for at least one published or presented analysis linking wastewater signals to clinical case counts for a non-COVID pathogen
- Develop standard process for triaging wastewater signals into epidemiological action (alert, investigate, no action)

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Weekly NWSS Data Submissions** — via NWSS data portal; completeness target ≥95%
- **Monthly Quality Reports** — QC metrics, inhibition rates, data completeness
- **Quarterly Progress Narratives**
- **Epidemiological Integration Reports** — semi-annual with signal-to-action examples
- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance

## 6. Budget Requirements

Personnel should include at least 1.0 FTE environmental scientist or microbiologist. Subcontracts to wastewater utilities for co-sampling are allowable with documentation.
