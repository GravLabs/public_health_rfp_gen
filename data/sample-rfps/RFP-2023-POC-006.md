# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Point-of-Care Testing Network Expansion — Post-COVID Laboratory Decentralization
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2023-POC-006  
**Issue Date:** July 17, 2023  
**Federal Sponsor:** Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA)  
**HRSA Award Number:** U68HS030229-01  
**Program Area:** Laboratory Access and Health Equity

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated that centralized public health laboratory testing creates bottlenecks during surge events and creates geographic inequities in access. Point-of-care (POC) testing — rapid, waived-complexity tests performed outside a traditional laboratory — emerged as a critical tool for underserved communities and rural settings where specimen transport time undermines the clinical utility of centralized testing.

Public Health Labs, through a HRSA cooperative agreement focused on laboratory access and health equity, is funding state public health laboratories to build oversight, quality assurance, and data integration infrastructure for POC testing networks. This is distinct from funding POC devices themselves — the focus is on the laboratory system infrastructure to make decentralized testing safe and data-complete.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state or large local public health laboratory
- Demonstrated existing or planned relationship with at least one Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) or Rural Health Clinic (RHC) in the state
- CLIA COLA or equivalent oversight authority or formal agreement with oversight entity
- State health department commitment letter supporting POC network expansion

Priority given to laboratories in states with high rural population proportion (>25% rural by Census definition) or high uninsured rate (>12%).

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. POC Network Registry and CLIA Oversight Support
- Develop or expand state POC testing site registry covering at least 50 active CLIA-waived sites
- Provide CLIA waiver compliance support to FQHCs and RHCs (site visits, remote assistance)
- Implement quality assurance monitoring protocol for waived testing sites

### B. Data Integration
- Implement electronic result capture from POC sites using SMART on FHIR or equivalent standards
- Achieve electronic reporting of POC results for at least 3 reportable conditions to state surveillance systems
- Establish data completeness metrics with quarterly reporting targets

### C. Equity Analysis and Program Adaptation
- Conduct baseline access analysis mapping POC site distribution against Social Vulnerability Index (SVI)
- Identify testing deserts and develop targeted outreach plan to add at least 10 new FQHC/RHC sites
- Report quarterly on testing volume by ZIP code with SVI overlay

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Equity Dashboard Submission** — semi-annual SVI-linked testing access map

- **Final Report** — 90 days post-period of performance

## 6. Budget Requirements
