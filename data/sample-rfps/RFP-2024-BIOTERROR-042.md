**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-BIOTERROR-042  
**Issue Date:** January 8, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / CSELS / OID  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-LRN-B  
**Program Area:** Bioterrorism Preparedness — Laboratory Response Network

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The Laboratory Response Network for Biological Threats (LRN-B) has been the backbone of bioterrorism detection in the United States since 1999. LRN-B Reference Laboratories at state public health laboratories perform confirmatory testing for Category A-C select agents and conduct surge testing during suspicious powder events, anthrax scares, and biological threat incidents. Post-COVID, LRN-B surge capacity has eroded: staffing turnover during the pandemic depleted trained personnel, equipment has aged, and recent tabletop exercises (TOPOFF and state-level exercises) have identified gaps in documentation, personnel qualification, and instrument validation. This RFP funds LRN-B Reference Laboratories to modernize threat agent detection capability, restore surge capacity, and update qualifications and SOPs to current CDC LRN-B standards.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Active CDC-recognized LRN-B Reference Laboratory
- CLIA high-complexity certification and all applicable USDA and CDC select agent registration
- Documented post-COVID capacity gap in staffing, equipment, or qualification status (signed by laboratory director)
- Passing score on most recent CDC LRN-B proficiency tests within last 24 months

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Personnel Qualification Restoration
- Achieve full qualification of minimum 4 laboratory scientists in all LRN-B Reference Laboratory assays (Bacillus anthracis, Yersinia pestis, Francisella tularensis, orthopoxvirus minimum) per CDC LRN-B qualification standards
- Complete CDC-required biosafety competency documentation for all personnel performing Tier 1 select agent testing within 6 months of award
- Develop and implement succession plan for qualified LRN-B personnel with at least 2 backfill-qualified staff per critical assay

### B. Instrumentation and Reagent Modernization
- Replace or validate any real-time PCR instrument exceeding 7 years of service age for LRN-B threat agent assays
- Restock LRN-B reagent stockpile to minimum 200 test equivalents per Category A agent assay panel
- Implement positive pressure glovebox or updated BSC configuration if current containment configuration does not meet current CDC LRN-B standards

### C. Surge Testing Capacity
- Demonstrate capability to process 200 environmental (powder) specimens per 24-hour period during a bioterrorism incident declaration
- Implement 24/7 on-call testing activation protocol with documented ≤2 hour activation time
- Conduct at least one internal surge exercise per year; document results and corrective actions

### D. Documentation and SOP Updates
- Update all LRN-B SOPs to reflect current CDC LRN-B methods revision (2023 revision minimum)
- Implement electronic laboratory notebook (ELN) or equivalent for LRN-B testing chain-of-custody documentation
- Complete all CDC LRN-B corrective actions from most recent external assessment within 12 months
