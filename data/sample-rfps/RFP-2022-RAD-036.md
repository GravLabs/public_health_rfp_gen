**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2022-RAD-036  
**Issue Date:** March 14, 2022  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / NCEH / Radiation Studies Branch  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU38EH001284-02  
**Program Area:** Radiation Emergency — Laboratory Response

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Nuclear and radiological threats — from dirty bombs and nuclear power plant accidents to improvised nuclear devices (INDs) — require specialized laboratory response capabilities that most state public health laboratories do not currently possess. The Laboratory Response Network for Radiation (LRN-R) is the national framework for coordinating such response, but membership requires validated capabilities in radiation bioassay and dose reconstruction. Post-COVID emergency preparedness funds present an opportunity to build this infrastructure. This RFP funds state laboratories to join or strengthen their LRN-R capacity through validated radiological testing capability, personnel dosimetry support, and urine/blood bioassay testing for radiation-exposed populations.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with existing radionuclide or chemistry testing capability

- Active participation in state emergency operations planning (SEOP) for radiological incidents
- Memorandum of understanding with state emergency management agency

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. LRN-R Membership and Validation
- Apply for and achieve LRN-R Level II or Level III membership within 18 months of award
- Complete all CDC LRN-R validation proficiency tests for urine bioassay (tritium, I-131, Cs-137, Sr-90)
- Achieve ANSI/HPS N13.30-2011 performance criteria for all validated bioassay tests

### B. Bioassay Laboratory Capability
- Implement validated methods for: urinary tritium (LSC), urinary iodine-131 (gamma spectrometry), whole body counting or in-vitro bioassay for cesium and strontium
- Maintain minimum 200 bioassay analyses per 24-hour surge period capacity
- Process routine and emergency bioassay samples with 24-hour turnaround for priority specimens

### C. Dose Reconstruction Support
- Partner with state and local health departments to support dose assessment for radiation-exposed populations
- Train minimum 2 laboratory staff in radiation dose reconstruction protocols
- Maintain contact with CDC Radiation Emergency Medical Management (REMM) and REAC/TS (Radiation Emergency Assistance Center and Training Site)

### D. Quality Assurance and Preparedness
- Participate in annual LRN-R readiness exercises and tabletop drills
- Submit quarterly QA data to CDC LRN-R coordinator
- Maintain proficiency through CDC LRN-R external quality assurance program
