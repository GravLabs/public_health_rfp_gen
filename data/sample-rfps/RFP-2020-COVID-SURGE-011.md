# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## COVID-19 Diagnostic Testing Surge Capacity — Emergency Supplemental
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2020-COVID-011  
**Issue Date:** April 3, 2020  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / COVID-19 Response  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-COVID-01  
**Program Area:** COVID-19 Emergency Response — Diagnostic Testing

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The United States declared a national public health emergency due to COVID-19 on January 31, 2020. State public health laboratories were among the first authorized to perform SARS-CoV-2 diagnostic testing using the CDC-developed rRT-PCR assay under Emergency Use Authorization. As community transmission expanded exponentially through March 2020, testing capacity became the critical bottleneck limiting public health response.

Public Health Labs is distributing CDC emergency supplemental funds on an expedited basis to state public health laboratories to immediately expand SARS-CoV-2 testing throughput, procure critical reagents, and establish specimen collection site networks. Given the declared public health emergency, standard competitive review timelines are compressed and awards may be made within 5 business days of application.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current Public Health Labs member state, territorial, or large local public health laboratory
- Authorized to perform SARS-CoV-2 rRT-PCR testing under CLIA certification or Emergency Use Authorization
- Capacity to receive and process nasopharyngeal/oropharyngeal specimens
- Ability to report results to state epidemiologists within 24 hours of result availability

All 50 states, Washington D.C., Puerto Rico, and U.S. territories are eligible. Applications from multiple laboratories within the same state must be coordinated through the state health department.

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Immediate Throughput Expansion
- Increase daily SARS-CoV-2 testing capacity to minimum 500 specimens/day within 30 days of award
- Implement high-throughput extraction platforms (e.g., Roche MagNA Pure, Hamilton, JANUS) to reduce manual processing bottlenecks
- Establish or expand 24/7 operations for testing during surge periods

### B. Reagent Security and Supply Chain
- Procure minimum 30-day supply of all critical reagents (RNA extraction, rRT-PCR master mix, swabs, VTM)
- Establish backup supplier relationships for at least 2 critical reagent categories
- Participate in Public Health Labs daily reagent inventory survey

### C. Specimen Collection Network
- Establish drive-through or community specimen collection sites in partnership with state emergency management; minimum 3 sites per state
- Provide training and collection supplies to at least 20 healthcare providers submitting specimens
- Implement electronic specimen manifest to eliminate paper chain-of-custody

### D. Reporting Integration
- Achieve electronic result reporting to CDC COVID-19 surveillance system within 24 hours of result finalization
- Report aggregate daily testing volumes to Public Health Labs Emergency Operations Center by 5 PM daily

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

- **Daily Operational Sitreps** — testing volumes, positivity rate, reagent inventory — due 5 PM daily to Public Health Labs EOC
- **Weekly Financial Reports** — expenditure to date, projected burn rate
- **Monthly Progress Narratives** — capacity milestones, supply chain issues, workforce status
- **Final Report** — 45 days post-period of performance

## 6. Budget Requirements

High-throughput extraction instruments allowable as capital equipment (pre-approval waived for this emergency award). Overtime pay allowable for 24/7 surge operations. Reagents, PPE, and specimen supplies allowable. Subcontracts to commercial reference laboratories for overflow testing allowable with documentation.
