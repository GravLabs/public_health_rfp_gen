## 1. Background and Purpose

Sepsis kills approximately 270,000 Americans annually and is the most expensive condition treated in U.S. hospitals, accounting for over $24 billion in healthcare costs. Early, accurate pathogen identification enables targeted antibiotic therapy, reducing mortality, shortening stays, and slowing antimicrobial resistance development. Rapid blood culture diagnostic systems (e.g., BioFire FilmArray BCID2, Verigene, T2Biosystems) and matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization time-of-flight (MALDI-TOF) mass spectrometry can identify pathogens and AMR markers within hours of blood culture positivity. State public health laboratory partnerships with acute care hospitals can expand access to these technologies and enable population-level sepsis surveillance.

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Rapid Diagnostic Implementation or Validation
- Implement or validate MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry for blood culture isolate identification (if not yet implemented): achieve species-level ID within 90 minutes of subculture
- Implement or validate rapid multiplex syndromic panel (BioFire BCID2 or equivalent) at state laboratory or partner hospital: achieve pathogen and AMR gene reporting within 5 hours of blood culture positivity
- Validate rapid ID for Candida species (MALDI-TOF or equivalent) with resistance marker detection for C. auris

### B. Sepsis Pathogen and AMR Surveillance
- Implement systematic collection of blood culture pathogen data from ≥3 partner hospital sites for state-level sepsis epidemiology
- Report sepsis pathogen distribution and AMR rates (MRSA, VRE, ESBL-producing Enterobacterales, KPC) quarterly to CDC NHSN
- Contribute isolates of epidemiological significance (unusual AMR patterns, outbreak clusters) to state public health laboratory for confirmation and WGS

### C. Antimicrobial Stewardship Laboratory Support
- Provide on-call consultation for partner hospitals regarding unusual susceptibility patterns or positive AMR molecular markers
- Implement rapid communication protocol: report blood culture rapid diagnostic results to clinical team within 30 minutes of result availability
- Track time-to-optimal antibiotic therapy metrics in partnership with at least 2 hospital stewardship programs

### D. Outreach and Training
- Train clinical laboratory staff at partner hospitals on rapid diagnostic result interpretation and stewardship implications
- Develop a rapid sepsis diagnostic interpretation guide for clinical pharmacists and infectious disease physicians
