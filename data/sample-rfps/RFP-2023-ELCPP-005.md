**Program Area:** ELC — Laboratory Systems and Workforce

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The Epidemiology and Laboratory Capacity for Prevention and Control (ELC) program provides foundational funding to state, territorial, and local health departments to build capacity in epidemiology, laboratory, and informatics. The laboratory component of ELC focuses on sustaining and modernizing the public health laboratory infrastructure that COVID-19 revealed to be under-resourced and technologically outdated.

This RFP represents Year 1 of a multi-year ELC Laboratory Systems funding opportunity. Projects funded in this cycle are expected to lay the groundwork for sustainable improvements that will continue beyond the immediate federal investment period.

## 4. Priority Areas and Scope of Work

### Priority Area A: Laboratory Information System Modernization
- Replace or significantly upgrade legacy LIMS systems to support real-time, bidirectional data exchange
- Implement HL7 FHIR R4 compliant ELR for all reportable conditions currently requiring manual reporting
- Achieve electronic case reporting (eCR) capability for at least 5 priority conditions by end of Period 1

### Priority Area B: Automation and Throughput
- Implement pre-analytical automation (specimen processing, aliquoting) to increase capacity by minimum 30% without adding FTEs
- Validate and implement at least one multiplexed syndromic testing panel (respiratory, gastrointestinal, or STI)
- Reduce average specimen-to-result turnaround time by 20% measured against baseline

### Priority Area C: Interoperability and Data Integration
- Integrate laboratory data systems with state health information exchange (HIE)
- Implement CDC MicrobeTrace or equivalent molecular epidemiology platform for at least one pathogen
- Participate in Public Health Labs's Informatics Messaging Services (AIMS) pilot for PHLIP integration

### Priority Area D: Workforce Pipeline Development
- Develop a formal laboratory scientist apprenticeship or internship program in partnership with at least one regional academic institution
- Implement competency-based performance evaluation framework for laboratory staff
- Fund and document completion of Public Health Labs professional development courses for 80% of laboratory staff

- **Financial Status Reports** — semi-annual via PMS

- **Milestone Completion Reports** — within 30 days of completing each Work Plan milestone
- **Final Performance Report** — 90 days post-period of performance

## 6. Budget Requirements

Equipment purchases over $5,000 require itemization in budget. LIMS vendor contracts must include performance and data portability provisions. Personnel must be dedicated to ELC activities proportional to effort stated in application. Subcontracts must be competitively procured per 2 CFR 200.
