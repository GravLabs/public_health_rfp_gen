## Food Safety Modernization Act Laboratory Support — STEC and Listeria Molecular Surveillance
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-FOODSAFE-047  
**Issue Date:** June 17, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Food and Drug Administration (FDA) / CFSAN  
**FDA Award Number:** FDA-CFSAN-2024-FSMA-028  
**Program Area:** Food Safety — FSMA Surveillance

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) shifted food safety from reactive to preventive, but public health laboratory capacity for molecular foodborne pathogen surveillance has not kept pace with FSMA's regulatory demands. STEC (Shiga toxin-producing E. coli) other than O157 — including O26, O103, O111, O121, O45, and O145 (the "Big Six") — are now reportable and increasingly linked to leafy green, produce, and flour outbreaks. Listeria monocytogenes WGS cluster detection through PulseNet has shortened outbreak detection from weeks to days but requires continuous sequencing capacity that many state laboratories cannot sustain. This RFP, administered through Public Health Labs in partnership with FDA CFSAN, funds state public health laboratories to achieve comprehensive STEC "Big Six" detection capability and sustained Listeria WGS surveillance.

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. STEC Big Six Detection Implementation
- Implement validated multiplex real-time PCR for simultaneous detection of stx1, stx2, eae, and all six non-O157 serogroup markers (O26, O45, O103, O111, O121, O145) per FDA BAM Chapter 4a or equivalent
- Achieve STEC Big Six detection for ≥95% of STEC-positive stool specimens within 48 hours of receipt
- Implement culture confirmation and STEC isolate recovery for ≥80% of stx-positive specimens for WGS submission to PulseNet

### B. Listeria Whole Genome Sequencing
- Achieve sustained Listeria monocytogenes WGS capacity of ≥15 isolates/week for routine surveillance
- Submit all clinical and food/environmental Listeria WGS results to PulseNet NCBI PathogenDB within 5 business days of culture confirmation
- Achieve allele concordance ≥99% with CDC PulseNet reference WGS laboratory on quarterly proficiency isolates

### C. Foodborne Outbreak Investigation Support
- Achieve 24-hour activation of enhanced foodborne pathogen testing during state or multistate outbreak investigation declaration
- Implement rapid PFGE-to-WGS pivot protocol: all Salmonella, STEC, and Listeria isolates from outbreak investigations WGS-sequenced within 72 hours
- Maintain direct communication channel with FDA CFSAN and CDC FoodNet for multistate outbreak clusters

### D. Food Testing and FSMA Support
- Provide limited food matrix STEC and Listeria testing support for state food safety regulatory investigations (not commercial testing)
- Validate STEC and Listeria detection methods in at least 2 food matrices (fresh produce and ready-to-eat deli meat) per FDA BAM
