**Issue Date:** October 3, 2022

**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000524-RSV  
**Program Area:** Respiratory Disease — RSV Surveillance

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) surged to unprecedented levels in fall 2022, overwhelming pediatric hospitals across the United States in a pattern driven by immunity debt accumulated during COVID-19 social distancing. RSV is the leading cause of hospitalization in U.S. infants and a significant cause of mortality in adults ≥65 and immunocompromised individuals. FDA approval of nirsevimab (Beyfortus) for infant RSV prevention and mRNA RSV vaccines for adults over 60 in 2023 creates new surveillance obligations to measure real-world vaccine impact.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to stand up sentinel RSV surveillance capable of detecting RSV timing, severity, and genotypic evolution across the age spectrum, contributing to CDC's RSV-NET and RESP-NET surveillance platforms.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state laboratory with RSV PCR capability
- CLIA high-complexity certification
- Partnership with at least one pediatric hospital and one long-term care or adult hospital for sentinel surveillance
- Agreement to contribute data to CDC RSV-NET or RESP-NET if state is a platform participant

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Sentinel RSV Surveillance
- Establish age-stratified RSV sentinel surveillance covering pediatric (<5 years), adult (18–64), and elder (≥65) populations
- Process minimum 50 RSV specimens per week during peak season (November–March)
- Achieve 5-day turnaround from sentinel site specimen collection to finalized laboratory result

### B. Genotypic Characterization
- Implement RSV-A and RSV-B genotyping by sequencing of the G protein attachment gene for minimum 10% of RSV-positive specimens
- Submit RSV sequences to NCBI Genbank and CDC within 30 days of sequencing
- Track and report clade distribution changes that may affect vaccine effectiveness

### C. Vaccine Impact Monitoring
- Establish infant RSV surveillance tracking nirsevimab receipt and RSV hospitalization outcomes in partnership with state immunization program
- Contribute RSV-NET data for vaccine effectiveness studies if state is a participating site
- Report RSV severity data (hospitalization, ICU admission, death) monthly

### D. Rapid Season Activation
- Achieve full RSV sentinel surveillance operational status by October 1 of each year
- Stockpile minimum 200 RSV PCR reactions for surge capacity
- Implement automated reporting to state epidemiology within 24 hours of sentinel positive result
