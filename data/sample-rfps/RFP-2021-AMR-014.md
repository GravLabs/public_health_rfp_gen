## 1. Background and Purpose

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a global health threat responsible for an estimated 35,000 deaths annually in the United States. The Antimicrobial Resistance Laboratory Network (ARLN), coordinated by CDC and Public Health Labs, supports state and local public health laboratories to detect and track resistant organisms including carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales (CRE), carbapenem-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa (CRPA), carbapenem-resistant Acinetobacter baumannii (CRAB), and emerging threats such as Candida auris and pan-resistant organisms.

This RFP funds ARLN member laboratories to sustain and expand capacity for detecting novel resistance mechanisms, performing WGS on priority AMR organisms, and integrating findings into regional and national AMR surveillance.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current ARLN member laboratory (Regional, Interim, or Surge capacity designation)
- CLIA high-complexity certification with capability for broth microdilution MIC testing
- Carbapenemase detection capability (Carba NP, mCIM, or equivalent) or commitment to implement within 90 days
- Active participation in ARLN data reporting portal
- Priority: laboratories in states with documented CRE, Candida auris, or CRPA healthcare outbreaks in prior 12 months

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Novel Resistance Mechanism Detection
- Implement or validate modified carbapenem inactivation method (mCIM) and EDTA-mCIM for all CRE isolates
- Implement lateral flow immunoassay (OXA-48, KPC, MBL detection) for rapid carbapenemase screening
- Report all carbapenemase-producing organisms to state health department and CDC ARLN within 24 hours of confirmed detection

### B. WGS for AMR Organism Characterization
- Perform WGS on 100% of novel carbapenemase producers and all Candida auris isolates
- Submit WGS data to CDC ARLN BioProject and NCBI within 14 days of sequencing
- Use AMRFinder or equivalent bioinformatics tool to characterize resistance gene content

### C. Healthcare Facility Support and Response
- Provide AMR consultation to healthcare facilities within the state upon request
- Respond to ARLN outbreak activation requests within 24 hours with agreed-upon testing services
- Conduct at least 2 tabletop exercises with regional healthcare coalitions during the period of performance

### D. Candida auris Surveillance
- Accept and screen environmental and patient surveillance specimens for Candida auris from at least 5 healthcare facilities
- Implement Candida auris speciation by MALDI-TOF MS or PCR within 48 hours of specimen receipt
- Report confirmed cases to CDC mycosis program within 24 hours
