## LRN-Chemical: Environmental and Clinical Specimen Testing Capacity
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2023-CHEM-007  
**Issue Date:** March 3, 2023  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / NCEH  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU90TP922072-07  
**Program Area:** Laboratory Response Network — Chemical (LRN-C)

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The Laboratory Response Network — Chemical (LRN-C) maintains a network of state public health and environmental laboratories capable of detecting chemical threat agents in clinical and environmental specimens. Following COVID-19, the LRN-C suffered attrition in trained chemical testing staff and lapse of proficiency in some network laboratories due to resource reallocation during the pandemic.

This RFP funds LRN-C Level 1 and Level 2 laboratories to rebuild chemical threat agent testing capacity, validate updated analytical methods, and integrate environmental specimen testing (air, water, soil) with clinical specimen workflows to support both terrorism response and environmental health surveillance use cases.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Current LRN-C Level 1 or Level 2 member laboratory
- LC-MS/MS or GC-MS/MS capability for trace-level chemical detection
- Current proficiency with Public Health Labs/CDC LRN-C proficiency test panels (passing score required for prior two test events)
- DEA Schedule I/II registration for controlled substance reference standards (or commitment to obtain within 90 days)

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Method Revalidation and Modernization
- Revalidate or update methods for Tier 1 chemical threat agents (nerve agents, blister agents, blood agents) using current CDC reference methods
- Implement isotope dilution mass spectrometry (IDMS) for at least 3 threat agent classes
- Achieve LOD at or below CDC action levels for each validated agent

### B. Environmental Specimen Integration
- Validate specimen preparation workflows for environmental matrices (water, wipe samples, air filter extracts) in addition to clinical matrices (urine, blood, serum)
- Develop chain-of-custody and sample integrity protocols for environmental specimens received from field responders
- Conduct at least one joint exercise with state emergency management and hazmat teams

### C. Workforce Reconstitution
- Document staffing plan to ensure at least 2 cross-trained analysts per shift for chemical threat testing
- Complete Public Health Labs/CDC LRN-C advanced training for all primary analysts within 12 months of award
- Develop and test on-call surge staffing protocol
