## Legionella Environmental Testing and Outbreak Investigation Capacity
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2023-LEGIONELLA-021  
**Issue Date:** January 9, 2023  
**Federal Sponsor:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / NCEZID  
**CDC Award Number:** 6NU50CK000524-02  
**Program Area:** Waterborne Disease — Legionella

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

Legionnaires' disease incidence has nearly tripled in the United States over the past two decades, driven by aging building water systems, increasing immunocompromised populations, and expanding hot tub and cooling tower use. Legionella pneumophila is the leading cause of waterborne disease outbreaks in the United States by mortality. Public health laboratory capacity for Legionella testing — both clinical diagnosis and environmental investigation — is uneven across states, creating delays in outbreak detection and source identification.

This RFP funds state public health laboratories to implement or expand environmental Legionella testing capability, integrate with healthcare outbreak response systems, and conduct sequence-based typing (SBT) to link clinical and environmental isolates during outbreak investigations.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

- Public Health Labs member state public health laboratory
- CLIA certification for clinical Legionella testing (culture, DFA, or urinary antigen) OR commitment to implement
- NELAP accreditation for environmental water testing OR commitment within 12 months
- Partnership with state environmental health or epidemiology program

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. Environmental Legionella Testing
- Implement and validate ISO 11731 or equivalent culture method for Legionella in water samples (cooling towers, hot water systems, potable water)
- Achieve NELAP accreditation for environmental Legionella culture within 12 months of award
- Accept environmental samples from healthcare facilities, hotels, and public buildings during outbreak investigations with ≤5-day turnaround

### B. Sequence-Based Typing for Outbreak Investigation
- Implement Legionella SBT (EWGLI/ESGLI protocol) for all clinical and environmental isolates from outbreak settings
- Submit SBT profiles to EWGLI international database within 30 days of completion
- Provide SBT-based source attribution analysis within 7 days of receiving matched clinical-environmental isolates

### C. Clinical Laboratory Support
- Offer Legionella urinary antigen testing (UAT) and culture for clinical specimens from the state at no cost to uninsured patients during outbreaks
- Report confirmed Legionella cases to state epidemiology within 24 hours

### D. Building Water System Program Support
- Provide technical consultation to healthcare facilities developing Water Management Plans (WMPs) per CDC MMWR guidance
- Conduct at least 2 environmental site visits to healthcare facilities in the state annually
