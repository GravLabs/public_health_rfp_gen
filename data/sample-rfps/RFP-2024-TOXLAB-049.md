# REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
## Clinical Toxicology Modernization — Drug Monitoring, Fentanyl Analogs, and Xylazine
**RFP ID:** Public Health Labs-RFP-2024-TOXLAB-049  
**Issue Date:** October 7, 2024  
**Federal Sponsor:** Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) / CSAP  
**SAMHSA Award Number:** SP-2024-TOXLAB-019  
**Program Area:** Toxicology — Opioid and Drug Monitoring

---

## 1. Background and Purpose

The U.S. drug overdose crisis claimed over 107,000 lives in 2023, driven by illicitly manufactured fentanyl (IMF) and an increasingly complex adulterant supply including nitazenes, xylazine (a veterinary alpha-2 agonist causing skin necrotic wounds resistant to naloxone), and novel benzodiazepines. State public health and toxicology laboratories must continuously update their testing capabilities to keep pace with the evolving drug supply. Traditional immunoassay-based drug screens miss novel synthetic opioids and xylazine; mass spectrometry confirmation panels must be routinely expanded. This RFP funds state toxicology laboratories to achieve comprehensive novel psychoactive substance (NPS) detection through LC-MS/MS and GC-MS platform expansion, post-mortem toxicology standardization, and drug checking service laboratory support.

- Partnership with state medical examiner/coroner system for post-mortem toxicology standardization
- Active participation in CDC Drug Overdose Surveillance and Epidemiology (DOSE) system or equivalent

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### A. NPS Detection Panel Expansion
- Expand LC-MS/MS confirmation panel to include minimum 200 analytes covering: fentanyl and ≥20 fentanyl analogs, nitazenes (isotonitazene, metonitazene, brorphine), xylazine, novel designer benzodiazepines (bromazolam, clonazolam, etizolam), and synthetic cannabinoids
- Validate expanded panel per SWGTOX or SOFT/AAFS guidelines with documented LOD/LOQ for each analyte
- Achieve semi-annual panel updates to include newly emerging NPS within 90 days of CDC NPS advisory

### B. Xylazine Surveillance
- Implement validated xylazine detection in urine, blood, and post-mortem matrices
- Report xylazine positivity rates and co-detection with opioids monthly to state overdose surveillance system
- Partner with wound care programs and harm reduction organizations to collect biological specimens from patients with xylazine-related skin wounds for toxicological confirmation

### C. Post-Mortem Toxicology Standardization
- Implement SOFT-recommended post-mortem toxicology specimen collection protocols (minimum: peripheral blood, vitreous humor, liver) in partnership with ≥3 county medical examiner offices
- Standardize cause-of-death toxicology reporting for opioid-involved deaths using CDC DOSE case definition
- Achieve turnaround of ≤21 days for all opioid overdose post-mortem toxicology cases

### D. Drug Checking Service Support
- Develop laboratory-based drug checking service in partnership with minimum 2 harm reduction organizations: accept street drug samples (pill, powder), perform LC-MS/MS identification, report results within 48 hours
- Implement secure, de-identified specimen submission protocol compliant with 21 CFR Part 1300 controlled substance exemptions for public health purposes
- Publish quarterly NPS drug supply reports for state policymakers and harm reduction community
